Customers can return an item within 30 days of delivery. To process a return, **proof of purchase** is required. A receipt or order confirmation is required. Documentation proving purchase is required. Proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation) must be provided.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days.

Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Faulty goods are outside the normal return window.
