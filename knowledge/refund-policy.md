## Return window

Customers can return an item within 30 days of delivery.

## Proof of purchase

To process a return, proof of purchase (a receipt or order confirmation) is required.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days.

## Item condition

Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods are outside the normal return window.
